Why Jatayu is on people's minds again

The Ramayana is back in the spotlight. Jatayu's story lives here.

A new Ramayana film has renewed public interest in Jatayu — the noble bird-king who tried to save Sita. Chadayamangalam is the place that tradition holds him to have fallen, and Jatayu Earth's Centre was already a recognised Kerala tourist attraction long before the film.

A note on accuracy: the film has renewed interest in Jatayu's story. This website does not claim the film was shot at Chadayamangalam, and is independent of the film and its makers.

ITHE LEGEND
Jatayu · the Ramayana
A story carried through generations

The story
of Jatayu.

In the Ramayana, Jatayu is the aged king of the vultures, a friend of Rama's father Dasharatha. When he sees Ravana carrying Sita away, he does not hesitate. Chadayamangalam — also called Jatayumangalam — is the place where, by local tradition, his flight ended.

I · The Abduction

Sita is taken

Ravana carries Sita southward in his aerial chariot, her cries echoing over the forest.

II · The Cry

Jatayu answers

Old and weary, the vulture-king hears her call and rises into the sky to stop Ravana.

III · The Battle

Sky against sword

Jatayu fights with all that remains of his strength before Ravana cuts away his wings.

IV · The Fall

Jatayu Paara

Local tradition holds that the wounded Jatayu fell upon the rocks of Chadayamangalam. The epic tells that he lived long enough to tell Rama what had happened to Sita.

“He did not win the battle. He is remembered because he chose to fight.”The spirit of the Jatayu legend
IIJATAYU PAARA
Jatayu Earth's Centre · Chadayamangalam
Jatayu Earth's Centre

A landmark
on the hill.

Jatayu Paara — the rock of Jatayu — rises above Chadayamangalam. On it stands Jatayu Earth's Centre, a hilltop tourism park built around a monumental sculpture of the wounded Jatayu, widely described as the largest bird sculpture in the world.

Visitors can climb the hill on foot or ride the cable car, step inside the sculpture, and look out over the Kerala countryside from the place where the legend is rooted.

Quick answers

Good to know.

The questions visitors ask most about Jatayu, Jatayu Paara and Chadayamangalam.

Where did Jatayu fall in the Ramayana?

According to local tradition, Jatayu fell on the rock now known as Jatayu Paara in Chadayamangalam, Kollam district, Kerala. Other places in India also claim associations with Jatayu's fall, which is common with ancient epics; Chadayamangalam's tradition is among the best known.

Where is Jatayu Paara?

In Chadayamangalam, Kollam district, Kerala (PIN 691534), on the M.C. Road corridor — roughly 38 km from Kollam and 46 km from Thiruvananthapuram.

Was the new Ramayana film shot at Jatayu Earth's Centre?

We have no information that it was, and this website does not claim so. The film has renewed public interest in Jatayu's story; Chadayamangalam is the place of the legend rather than a film location.

What is Jatayu Earth's Centre?

A hilltop tourism park at Jatayu Paara featuring a monumental Jatayu sculpture, a cable car, a hilltop walkway of 826 steps, a museum and adventure activities.

What else can I see in Chadayamangalam?

Beyond Jatayu Paara: the Ithikkara River landscape, local temples and heritage, rocky hills and countryside, and everyday village life along the M.C. Road.

What are the opening hours and ticket prices?

These can change, so please check the official Jatayu Earth's Centre website before you travel.
