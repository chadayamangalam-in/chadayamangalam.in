Jatayu Earth's Centre after the Ramayana film: what to know before you visit

14 November 20265 min readChadayamangalam.in

A new Ramayana film has put Jatayu — the noble bird-king who tried to save Sita — back in conversation across India. For many people, the next question is: where did Jatayu fall? In Kerala, the answer tradition gives is Chadayamangalam.

An important clarification. The film has renewed interest in Jatayu's story and in the places associated with it. We are not saying the film was shot at Chadayamangalam, and this website has no connection with the film or its makers. Jatayu Earth's Centre was already an established tourist attraction long before the film's release.

Where did Jatayu fall in the Ramayana?

In the Ramayana, Jatayu sees Ravana carrying Sita away and challenges him in the sky. After a fierce fight, Ravana cuts away his wings and the old vulture-king falls wounded. The epic tells that he lived long enough to tell Rama what had happened to Sita.

According to local tradition in Kerala, the place where Jatayu fell is Jatayu Paara — the "rock of Jatayu" — in Chadayamangalam, Kollam district. The village is also known as Jatayumangalam. As with many places linked to ancient epics, other parts of India also claim associations with Jatayu, so it is best described as a belief held by local tradition rather than a proven site.

What is Jatayu Paara in Kollam?

Jatayu Paara is a rocky hill rising above Chadayamangalam. On it stands Jatayu Earth's Centre, a hilltop tourism park built around a monumental sculpture of the wounded Jatayu — widely described as the largest bird sculpture in the world. Its approximate size is 200 feet in length, 150 feet in width and 70 feet in height.

Visitors can reach the top in two ways: a hilltop walkway of 826 steps, or a cable car. Alongside the sculpture there are story and museum experiences and outdoor adventure activities.

Chadayamangalam tourist attractions beyond Jatayu

Chadayamangalam rewards a longer stay than a quick photograph:

  • Jatayu Paara and the sculpture — the main landmark and the heart of the legend.
  • The Ithikkara River — the river that flows alongside the village and shapes its landscape.
  • Heritage and temples — traditional architecture and the stories of the region.
  • Rocky hills and countryside — long views across Kollam district.
  • Village life — local food, markets and festivals along the M.C. Road.

Planning your visit

  • Where: Chadayamangalam, Kollam district, Kerala 691534, on the M.C. Road corridor.
  • Distance: roughly 38 km from Kollam and 46 km from Thiruvananthapuram.
  • Timing: go early if you plan to climb the 826 steps, and avoid the heat of midday.
  • Before you go: opening hours, tickets and activities can change — please confirm on the official website.

Why Jatayu's story still matters

Jatayu does not win his battle. He is remembered because he chose to fight for someone else when he knew he was outmatched. That is why a story told for thousands of years can bring people to a rock in Kerala today — and why films, old and new, keep returning to it.
