Where did Jatayu fall in the Ramayana? 🦅

With the Ramayana back in the spotlight, many people are asking about Jatayu — the noble bird-king who fought to save Sita.

In Kerala, tradition says his story ends right here: Jatayu Paara, Chadayamangalam, Kollam.

Today it is home to Jatayu Earth's Centre — a hilltop park with a monumental Jatayu sculpture, a cable car and 826 steps to the top. It was a well-known Kerala attraction long before the film, and the film has now renewed interest in the legend behind it.

Our new guide covers where Jatayu fell, what to see at Jatayu Paara, and what else Chadayamangalam has to offer 👇
